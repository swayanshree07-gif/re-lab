# 19-vm-check: hints

Open one level at a time. Level 3 is close to a spoiler.

<details><summary>Level 1: Nudge</summary>

Registers now, and loops. Get the instruction length right first.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 2: Direction</summary>

Recover the opcode table, then disassemble. You'll see a loop over the input.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 3: Near-spoiler</summary>

Write a small emulator. Either invert the loop's per-byte transform or brute-force each byte by its compare result.

</details>
