# 06-serial-format: hints

Open one level at a time. Level 3 is close to a spoiler.

<details><summary>Level 1: Nudge</summary>

First pass the format check: count characters and dashes.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 2: Direction</summary>

Some characters are check characters computed from the others with weights.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 3: Near-spoiler</summary>

Pick random data characters, compute the check characters with the same weighted sum and modulus, and you have a keygen.

</details>
