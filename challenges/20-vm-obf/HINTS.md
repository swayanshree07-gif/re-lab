# 20-vm-obf: hints

Open one level at a time. Level 3 is close to a spoiler.

<details><summary>Level 1: Nudge</summary>

Same VM family as 19, but the opcodes are shuffled and the bytecode is encrypted.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 2: Direction</summary>

Find the decryption before dispatch, then work out the opcode map from what each handler does.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 3: Near-spoiler</summary>

Decrypt, remap the opcodes to 19's ISA, and reuse your 19 emulator/solver.

</details>
