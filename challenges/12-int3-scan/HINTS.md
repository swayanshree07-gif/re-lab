# 12-int3-scan: hints

Open one level at a time. Level 3 is close to a spoiler.

<details><summary>Level 1: Nudge</summary>

Breakpoints change the code bytes. Does the program look at its own code?

</details>

<details><summary>Level 2: Direction</summary>

A loop reads `.text` and both hashes it and counts `0xCC` bytes.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 3: Near-spoiler</summary>

Use hardware breakpoints (`hbreak`) or watchpoints, or solve it statically without breakpoints in the hashed range.

</details>
