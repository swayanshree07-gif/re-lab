# 18-vm-intro: hints

Open one level at a time. Level 3 is close to a spoiler.

<details><summary>Level 1: Nudge</summary>

A big `switch` in a loop is an interpreter. Find its bytecode.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 2: Direction</summary>

Map each opcode to a stack operation by reading its case.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 3: Near-spoiler</summary>

Write a disassembler for the bytecode. The checks are pushes and compares you can read directly.

</details>
